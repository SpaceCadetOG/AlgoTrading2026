# Volume Profile Real Trade Mapping

## Purpose

Map book trade examples to research artifacts without creating executable entries or exits.

- Daily open: `research/price_action_strategy_study.csv`
- Failed auction: `research/price_action_phase3_study.csv`
- VWAP behavior: `research/vwap_interaction.csv`

## Swing Mapping

- Accumulation: `research/volume_setup_accumulation.csv`
- Trend: `research/volume_setup_trend.csv`
- Rejection: `research/volume_setup_rejection.csv`
- Reversal: `research/volume_setup_reversal.csv`
- Combined features and labels: `research/setup_features_labels.csv`

## Long-Term Mapping

- Composite profile: `research/volume_profile_features.csv`
- 30-day profile shape: `research/volume_profile_shape_study.csv`

## Constraint

These mappings are for manual review and future research packets only.
