# Volume Profile Psychology

## Good Winner

A good winner follows the plan, respects the setup definition, and exits according to researched target logic.

## Bad Winner

A bad winner makes money by breaking rules. It reinforces behavior that can damage the process later.

## Good Loser

A good loser respects invalidation and preserves mental and financial capital.

## Bad Loser

A bad loser widens stops, adds outside the plan, ignores profile invalidation, or refuses to accept that the auction changed.

## Drawdown Handling

- Reduce review scope.
- Stop adding new setup logic.
- Re-check labels and assumptions.
- Separate research failure from execution failure.

## Rule-Breaking Prevention

The repo keeps research, labels, backtests, OMS, gateway, and execution separate so a research idea cannot accidentally become a live trade.
